About

More Than a Title. A Journey in Progress.

I am Md Mizanur Rahman, an entrepreneur from Bangladesh currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Engineering at Daffodil International University.

My journey brings together entrepreneurship, brand building, digital creativity, and technology. As the Founder of TyLora Attire Limited and Co-Founder of Tava Group, I am learning to turn ideas into practical businesses while developing my technical and leadership skills.

My interests extend beyond business. I enjoy graphic design, digital marketing, programming, data science, research, and exploring how technology can solve real-world problems.

I believe meaningful progress comes from learning, experimenting, solving problems, and continuing to build.
